DigitalSupplyChain.es / Reference architecture

Reference architecture

A technology-neutral conceptual stack for connecting operational systems, external partners, data models and decision support.

Arquitectura de referencia

Un stack conceptual neutral respecto a tecnología para conectar sistemas operacionales, socios externos, modelos de datos y soporte a decisiones.

01 SourcesERP, MES, WMS, TMS, QMS, PLM, supplier and logistics systems.
02 IntegrationAPIs, events, EDI, files, gateways and partner connectors.
03 Canonical dataIdentity, master data, events, relationships and provenance.
04 IntelligenceTrace queries, KPIs, risk models, alerts and graph analytics.
05 ExperienceControl-tower views, supplier portals, operations and lifecycle access.

Design principles

  • System of systemsAvoid assuming one application owns the entire supply-chain truth.
  • Event-first visibilityRepresent significant business events and transformations explicitly.
  • Stable identityResolve aliases and identifiers before attempting cross-system analytics.
  • Controlled sharingExpose only the context required by each actor and use case.

Typical analytics

  • Trace / genealogyWhere did this item come from and where did it go?
  • Exception detectionWhich expected event did not occur within the planned window?
  • Dependency analysisWhich products depend on this supplier, site or material?
  • Lifecycle contextWhich documents and attributes belong to this product or component?
