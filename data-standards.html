Data & interoperability

A conceptual view of the information structures that allow supply-chain systems to exchange identity, state, events and provenance.

Datos e interoperabilidad

Una visión conceptual de las estructuras de información que permiten a los sistemas de supply chain intercambiar identidad, estado, eventos y procedencia.

Core building blocks

Interoperability depends less on one platform than on consistent semantics, identifiers and event structures.

LayerPurposeTypical concepts
IdentityKnow precisely what entity a record refers to.Product, lot, serial, location, organization, asset identifiers
Master dataProvide relatively stable descriptive context.Supplier, material, BOM, site, lane, packaging hierarchy
EventsDescribe state changes and observations over time.Commission, receive, transform, aggregate, ship, inspect
DocumentsLink evidence and structured business records.Certificates, quality records, declarations, shipping documents
APIs & messagesExchange data between systems and organizations.REST/JSON, events, webhooks, queues, EDI mappings
GovernanceControl meaning, quality, access and lifecycle.Schema ownership, data contracts, lineage, retention, permissions

GS1 / EPCIS concepts

Useful conceptual patterns for event-based visibility: what, when, where, why and business context.

Digital Product Passport

A policy and data-sharing direction that increases the importance of structured lifecycle and product information.

Data contracts

Explicit schemas, definitions, ownership and compatibility rules reduce semantic drift between systems.
