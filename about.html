About the project

DigitalSupplyChain.es is an independent conceptual website exploring digital supply-chain architecture, traceability, interoperability and resilience.

Sobre el proyecto

DigitalSupplyChain.es es una web conceptual independiente sobre arquitectura de supply chain digital, trazabilidad, interoperabilidad y resiliencia.

Scope

The content is structured as a knowledge map: each page focuses on one capability and connects it to the wider digital thread.

Positioning

The site does not claim to operate a live software product. It is intended as a domain concept, reference hub and demonstration of how the information architecture could be structured.
